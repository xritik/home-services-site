Frequently asked questions

Yes, we use certified, child- and pet-safe formulations, and our technicians will advise on any precautions specific to the treatment.

Most treatments show visible results within 3&ndash;7 days, with a follow-up visit included to handle any residual activity.

Not for most treatments &mdash; your technician will let you know if any specific area needs to be avoided for a few hours.

Yes, treatments include a service warranty period during which a follow-up visit is included at no extra cost.
