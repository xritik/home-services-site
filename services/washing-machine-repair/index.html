Washing Machine Repair in Jaipur

NestCare technicians handle the full range of washing machine problems — from a machine that won't drain to one that won't spin — for front load, top load and semi-automatic models.

We repair and service all major brands, including LG, Samsung, IFB, Whirlpool and Bosch.

Services covered by our washing machine experts

  • Machine does not start
  • Not spinning or draining
  • Excess noise or vibration
  • Water leakage
  • Door lock / lid issues
  • Installation & un-installation
  • PCB / motor repair
  • General servicing

All about the service

Working procedure: our technician inspects the machine on-site, quotes a standard price, and starts work only once you approve it.

Service inclusion: the listed price covers labour only. General servicing covers issue identification and diagnosis; any repair is quoted separately before work begins.

Service exclusion: a ₹100 convenience fee applies if a technician needs to procure a spare part mid-job. Branded spare parts are charged at MRP.

Service warranty: NestCare covers the service itself for 30 days — this does not extend to spare parts.
