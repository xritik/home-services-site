Waterproofing in Jaipur

Waterproofing projects are handled by a dedicated coordinator from first visit to handover. We start with a free, no-obligation site visit so your quote reflects the actual space and material choices — not a generic estimate.

What's covered

  • Leakage source inspection
  • Terrace waterproofing
  • Bathroom & wet-area waterproofing
  • Wall seepage treatment
  • Crack filling & sealing
  • Membrane / coating application
  • Post-treatment testing
  • Warranty on treatment

All about the service

Working procedure: our technician inspects the job on-site, quotes a standard price, and starts work only once you approve it.

Service inclusion: the listed price covers labour only. General servicing covers issue identification and diagnosis; any repair is quoted separately before work begins.

Service warranty: NestCare covers the service itself for 30 days — this does not extend to spare parts or materials.

How pricing works

Waterproofing is quoted per project, since scope and material choices vary widely. Share a few details in the form and our team will schedule a free site visit to give you an itemized quote — there's no obligation to proceed after the visit.
