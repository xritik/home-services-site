All about the service

Working procedure: our technician inspects the unit on-site, quotes a standard price, and starts work only once you approve it.

Service inclusion: the listed price covers labour only. General servicing covers issue identification and diagnosis; any repair is quoted separately before work begins.

Service exclusion: a ₹100 convenience fee applies if a technician needs to procure a spare part mid-job (the job timer pauses during this). Branded spare parts are charged at MRP.

Service warranty: NestCare covers the service itself for 30 days — this does not extend to spare parts.
