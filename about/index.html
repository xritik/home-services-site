About us

Home services shouldn't feel like a gamble.

NestCare started with a simple frustration: finding a technician you can trust for something as basic as a leaking AC or a broken washing machine takes too many phone calls, too much guesswork, and too many unpleasant surprises on price.

We built a single platform where every technician is screened before they're allowed to take a booking, every price is quoted before work begins, and every job carries a 30-day service warranty. Today we cover appliance repair, deep cleaning, pest control, handyman work, and interior renovation across Jaipur.
